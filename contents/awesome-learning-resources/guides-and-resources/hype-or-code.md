Free, auto-updated gallery that gives every new AI model on OpenRouter the same six prompts (a pelican on a bicycle as SVG, an HTML wristwatch, a landing page, a playable Breakout game, a logo and About copy), one shot each, and shows the outputs side by side with the time and cost of every run. No scores, no signup.
